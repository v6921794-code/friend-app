Privacy Policy

Friend is a random communication platform designed for adults aged 18 and above.

Information We Collect

Friend may store account information such as your login identifier, date of birth, username and profile bio.

Passwords

Passwords are stored in hashed form and are not intentionally stored as plain text.

Camera and Microphone

Camera and microphone access is requested only when you choose to use video or audio features. You can revoke these permissions through your browser or device settings.

Reports and Blocks

Reports and blocking information may be stored to help maintain platform safety and moderation.

Third Parties

Do not share passwords, OTPs, addresses, school or college information, or other private information with strangers.

Changes

This policy may be updated as the service develops.
